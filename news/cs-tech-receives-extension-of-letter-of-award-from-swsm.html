CS TECH Ai received extension of Letter of Award from State Water and Sanitation Mission (SWSM), Uttar Pradesh

News 30 Nov 2023

Nagpur, Maharashtra, India, 30 November, 2023 – CS TECH Ai (Ceinsys Tech Ltd), a pioneer in providing Geospatial, IT, Engineering and Mobility services has received an extension of one of its major projects for Third Party Inspection (TPI) and Monitoring of Physical and Financial Progress through a Quality and Cost Based Selection (QCBS) process for various rural water supply infrastructure project from State Water and Sanitation Mission (SWSM), Uttar Pradesh.

The project originally awarded in the Financial year 2021-22 is now extended till 15th June, 2024. For more details, please refer: https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/1271f610-988c-44aa-827b-0f84b530595a.pdf
