CS TECH Ai order book crosses ₹1,000 Cr

News 9 Oct 2024

CS TECH Ai (Ceinsys Tech Ltd, BSE CODE: 538734) has received a Letter of Award (LOA) from State Water and Sanitation mission (SWSM) of Maharashtra State for value of Rs. 332 cr on 8th October, 2024. This refers to the implementation of IoT deployment including Design, Implementation and maintenance with Centralized IoT platform for Jal Jeevan Mission projects including maintenance for a period of 5 years.

This was in addition to recent LOA from CIDCO for a total value of Rs. 29.2 cr which included Drone survey, creation of base maps and utility layers and also the Design and development of Enterprise GIS application and providing related software for a bundled solution and also operations and maintenance programme for the said solution.

The company has now confirmed order book to be executed in excess of Rs. 1000 cr. This is a new milestone in the history of CS TECH Ai where it has continued to deliver qualitative solutions for its valued customers.

CS TECH Ai recent investments:

CS TECH Ai recently completed preferential allotment of equity shares and share warrants for a total fund raise of US $28 mn. In addition it recently acquired business assets of US-based Geospatial entity VTS and has also expanded its presence to Asia Pacific region by setting up wholly owned subsidiary to expand into Tech enabled services with aim to build significant presence in Data centre business by providing various services including design, development and technical consulting for setting up Data centre in and outside India.

CS TECH Ai plans grow geometrically and will lead the company to new horizon by consolidating its position in the Geospatial, Engineering and tech enabled serviced, thereby positioning itself as a complete solution provider to enable design, develop and monitor and operate large projects in Water, energy, Automobile, AEC and related Infrastructure enabling a sustainable growth of Rural and Urban India and also provide impetus to similar projects in USA, Europe and Southeast Asia.

About CS TECH Ai

Founded in 1998 by the Meghe group, CS TECH Ai has over the years focused on offering technology solutions across various domains and established itself as a premier Geospatial & Engineering solution provider in the ITES space.

The Geospatial engineering services and Enterprise solutions offerings encompass various aspects of geospatial intelligence, including Data Creation, Data Analytics, Decision Support Systems (DSS), Enterprise Web Solutions, and Dashboards.

CS TECH Ai is a technology-driven efficient solutions provider to satisfied customers across private and government enterprises across the utility, infrastructure, natural resources, and Manufacturing sectors. It has established since 1998 and now plans to grow geometrically to a new horizon by consolidating its position in the Geospatial, Engineering and tech enabled serviced, thereby positioning itself as a complete solution provider to enable design, develop and monitor and operate large projects in Water, energy, Automobile, AEC and related Infrastructure enabling a sustainable growth of Rural and Urban India and also provide impetus to similar projects in USA, Europe and Southeast Asia.

It has a global presence with offices in India, the United States, United Kingdom and Germany.

The company is also into software product development, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML) and Embedded Electronics space through a new vertical formation which focuses on product development activities related to Metaverse, Ed-Tech, Gaming and Mobility.
