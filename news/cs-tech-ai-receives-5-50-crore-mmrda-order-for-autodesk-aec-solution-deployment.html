CS TECH Ai Receives ₹5.50 Crore MMRDA Order for Autodesk AEC Solution Deployment

News 9 May 2025

Project to Enhance Digital Design and Engineering Capabilities for Infrastructure Development

May 9, 2025 – CS TECH Ai (Ceinsys Tech Limited) has received a Letter of Award (LoA) from the Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority for the supply, installation, and commissioning of Autodesk Architecture, Engineering and Construction (AEC) Collection IC Commercial ELD solutions for MMRDA. The order is valued at ₹5.50 crore.

The project represents another important milestone for CS TECH Ai in the field of digital engineering and infrastructure technology solutions. Through this engagement, the company will support MMRDA in strengthening its digital design, engineering, and project execution capabilities using advanced Autodesk AEC technologies.

The Autodesk AEC Collection is a comprehensive suite of software tools widely used across architecture, engineering, and construction industries for design development, Building Information Modeling (BIM), infrastructure planning, visualization, and project coordination. These solutions help organizations improve collaboration, reduce design conflicts, optimize workflows, and enhance overall project efficiency.

By implementing advanced digital engineering platforms, MMRDA is expected to enhance the management and execution of large-scale infrastructure and urban development projects across the Mumbai Metropolitan Region. The deployment of integrated design and construction technologies can improve project accuracy, streamline communication among stakeholders, and support faster decision-making throughout the project lifecycle.

The initiative also reflects the growing adoption of digital transformation and BIM-led workflows within the infrastructure sector. Technology-enabled planning and engineering solutions are increasingly becoming essential for delivering complex urban infrastructure projects with improved quality, transparency, and operational efficiency.

CS TECH Ai has developed strong expertise in geospatial technologies, engineering services, enterprise solutions, and digital infrastructure systems for government and industrial sectors. The latest order further strengthens the company’s relationship with MMRDA and reinforces its position as a trusted technology partner for smart infrastructure initiatives.
