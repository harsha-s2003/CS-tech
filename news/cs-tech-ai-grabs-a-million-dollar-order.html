CS TECH Ai bags a million-dollar order to implement a SCADA based flow monitoring system to facilitate thermal power station

News 4 Dec 2023

Nagpur, Maharashtra, India, 4 December, 2023 – CS TECH Ai (Ceinsys Tech Ltd), a pioneer in providing Geospatial, IT, Engineering and Mobility services has been appointed as the System Integrator (SI) for Implementation of Flow Monitoring System for Thermal Power Station under MAHAGENCO, (Maharashtra State Power Generation Company Limited) in the state of Maharashtra. CS TECH Ai will supply, install Electromagnetic flow meters of various diameters for 210 MW & 500 MW plant capacity at different sections of the plant and fetch the data in wireless PLC/RTU. Further all the data will be stored in centralized SCADA system. CS TECH Ai will also carry water audit for noting where water is launched into supply and where it leaves the system. The water audit will be performed for treated or untreated water transmission systems, distinct treated water distribution systems such as pressure districts etc.

The centralized monitoring will empower the authorities to make informed decisions and monitor the progress of the project effectively.

Valued at INR 9.06 Crores ($1.09 Mn), the project will be implemented in 1 year. This prestigious project is a testament to CS TECH Ai’s commitment to leveraging technology and innovation to solve real-world problems and create a sustainable future for all.
