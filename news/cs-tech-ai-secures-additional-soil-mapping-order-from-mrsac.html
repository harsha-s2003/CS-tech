CS TECH Ai Secures Additional Soil Mapping Order from MRSAC

News 29 November 2025

Strengthening Contribution to National Soil Mapping Programme in Maharashtra

November 29, 2025 – CS TECH Ai (Ceinsys Tech Limited) has received an additional work order from the Maharashtra Remote Sensing Application Centre (MRSAC) for conducting a Detailed Soil Survey and Mapping under the National Soil Mapping Programme (NSMP). The latest order pertains to a part of Nashik district in Maharashtra and is valued at Rs. 2.02 crore (inclusive of GST).

This development builds upon earlier work orders awarded to the company, as communicated in September and November 2025. With this addition, the cumulative value of contracts received by CS TECH Ai from MRSAC under the NSMP now stands at approximately Rs. 7.06 crore (inclusive of GST).

The company is empanelled with MRSAC for executing detailed soil survey and mapping projects, reflecting its technical expertise and proven capabilities in geospatial and environmental services.

The Detailed Soil Survey and Mapping initiative plays a crucial role in supporting sustainable agricultural practices and informed land-use planning. By generating accurate soil data and maps, the project enables better decision-making for farmers, policymakers, and agricultural planners.

It helps in identifying soil characteristics, fertility levels, and suitability for different crops, thereby contributing to improved productivity and resource management.

CS TECH Ai’s involvement in this programme highlights its commitment to leveraging advanced geospatial technologies for environmental and agricultural development. The project also reinforces the company’s growing presence in government-led initiatives focused on data-driven planning and sustainability.
