CS TECH Ai Receives ₹11.39 Crore PMC Order for Underground Drainage Project in Vasai-Virar

News 6 May 2025

Consultancy Assignment to Support Urban Sanitation and Wastewater Infrastructure Development

May 6, 2025 – CS TECH Ai (Ceinsys Tech Limited) has received a work order for providing Project Management Consultancy (PMC) services for the Underground Drainage System project of the Navghar-Manikpur STP Zone-6 area for the Vasai Virar City Municipal Corporation. The project is valued at ₹11.39 crore.

The assignment marks another significant development in CS TECH Ai’s growing portfolio of urban infrastructure and public utility projects. Through this consultancy engagement, the company will support the planning, coordination, supervision, and execution management of the underground drainage infrastructure within the designated zone.

Modern underground drainage systems are critical for effective wastewater management in rapidly growing urban regions. Such infrastructure helps improve sanitation standards, reduce waterlogging, support environmental protection, and strengthen public health systems. Efficient drainage networks also play a vital role in sustainable city development by ensuring proper collection, transportation, and treatment of wastewater.

The Navghar-Manikpur STP Zone-6 project is expected to contribute to strengthening sewage and wastewater management capabilities within the Vasai-Virar region. With increasing urbanization and population growth, cities require robust drainage and sanitation infrastructure to maintain environmental balance and improve quality of life for residents.

As part of the PMC role, CS TECH Ai is expected to utilize its expertise in engineering consultancy, infrastructure management, and digital project monitoring to support efficient project implementation. Professional project management consultancy services help ensure timely execution, quality control, regulatory compliance, and effective coordination among stakeholders involved in large-scale infrastructure projects.

The project further highlights the increasing demand for technology-driven and professionally managed urban infrastructure solutions across India. CS TECH Ai continues to strengthen its presence in sectors such as water management, geospatial solutions, engineering services, and smart infrastructure development.
