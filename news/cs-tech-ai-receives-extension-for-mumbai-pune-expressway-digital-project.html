CS TECH Ai Receives Extension for Mumbai–Pune Expressway Digital Project

News 1 January 2026

Continued Role in Advancing Smart Infrastructure for the Missing Link Project

January 1, 2026 – CS TECH Ai (Ceinsys Tech Limited) has been granted a further extension for the implementation of the Digital Project Management System for the Mumbai–Pune Expressway (MPEW) Missing Link (ML) project.

The extension has been awarded by MSRDC Tunnel Limited and is valued at approximately Rs. 12.06 crore (excluding GST).

This extension builds on the company’s ongoing engagement as a System Integrator for the project, a role it has held since the original contract awarded in FY 2020–21, followed by a previous extension in FY 2022–23.

Under this assignment, CS TECH Ai is responsible for the supply, implementation, and support of a comprehensive digital project management platform.

The platform integrates advanced technologies such as 5D Building Information Modelling (BIM), Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), and Geographic Information Systems (GIS). Together, these systems enable real-time project monitoring, efficient resource planning, and improved coordination across multiple stakeholders.

Additionally, CS TECH Ai will continue to support the establishment and operation of the Owner’s Support Office, ensuring streamlined communication and decision-making throughout the project lifecycle.

The Mumbai–Pune Expressway Missing Link project is a critical infrastructure initiative aimed at improving connectivity and reducing travel time between two major cities.

The adoption of digital project management tools plays a key role in enhancing execution efficiency, minimizing risks, and ensuring transparency in large-scale infrastructure development.

This continued association reflects the trust placed in CS TECH Ai’s technical expertise and its ability to deliver reliable, technology-driven solutions for complex infrastructure projects.
