CS TECH Ai JV Wins International GIS Project in Georgia

News 29 April 2026

Landmark Contract for Land Information System Strengthens Global Presence

April 29, 2026 – CS TECH Ai (Ceinsys Tech Limited), in joint venture with Magnasoft Consulting India Private Limited and GIS and RS Consulting Centre GeoGraphic LLC, has received a Letter of Acceptance from the Ministry of Environmental Protection and Agriculture (MEPA), Government of Georgia. The contract involves the design, supply, and installation of a comprehensive Land Information System (LIS) for the country.

The project, awarded on April 28, 2026, carries a total value of approximately Rs. 20.85 crore (inclusive of taxes), equivalent to USD 2.2 million. This milestone marks a significant step in CS TECH Ai’s international expansion and highlights its growing expertise in delivering large-scale geospatial and digital infrastructure solutions.

The Land Information System is expected to play a crucial role in improving land administration, enhancing data accuracy, and enabling better decision-making for environmental protection and agricultural planning. By integrating advanced GIS technologies, the system will support efficient land records management, transparency, and streamlined governance processes in Georgia.

This collaboration brings together the combined strengths of the joint venture partners, leveraging their technical capabilities and domain expertise to deliver a robust and future-ready solution. It also reinforces CS TECH Ai’s position as a trusted partner for global government projects focused on digital transformation and sustainable development.

In line with regulatory requirements, the company has disclosed the details of this contract in accordance with the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, along with the applicable SEBI Master Circular dated January 30, 2026. Further details are available in Annexure A for reference.

This achievement reflects CS TECH Ai’s commitment to delivering high-impact solutions and expanding its footprint in international markets.
