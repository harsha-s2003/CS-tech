CS TECH Ai Secures ISRO Subscription Renewal Contract

News 27 March 2026

Reinforcing Partnership Through Advanced Design and Manufacturing Solutions

March 27, 2026 – CS TECH Ai (Ceinsys Tech Limited) has been awarded a service order by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) for the renewal of its “Product Design & Manufacturing Collection Commercial Multi-user Subscription” for a period of three years.

The contract is valued at Rs. 3,24,50,000/- (Rupees Three Crore Twenty-Four Lakh Fifty Thousand Only), inclusive of GST.

This renewal highlights the continued trust that ISRO places in CS TECH Ai’s ability to deliver reliable and high-performance solutions for complex engineering environments. The subscription provides access to advanced tools that support product design, simulation, and manufacturing workflows, which are essential for precision-driven sectors such as aerospace.

Through this engagement, CS TECH Ai will continue to enable seamless collaboration among multiple users, ensuring efficiency and consistency in design processes. The multi-user environment supports faster development cycles, improved accuracy, and better integration across different stages of product development.

These capabilities are critical for organizations like ISRO, where innovation, quality, and speed are key to mission success.

The contract also strengthens CS TECH Ai’s presence in the aerospace and government sectors, reaffirming its role as a trusted technology partner. It reflects the company’s commitment to delivering scalable and future-ready solutions that meet the evolving needs of its clients.

In line with regulatory requirements, the details of this order have been disclosed in accordance with the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, along with the applicable SEBI Master Circular dated January 30, 2026. Further information has been provided in Annexure A for reference.

This development underscores CS TECH Ai’s ongoing focus on innovation, quality delivery, and maintaining transparency with stakeholders.
