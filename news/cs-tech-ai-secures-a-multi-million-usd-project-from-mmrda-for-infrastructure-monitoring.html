CS TECH Ai secures a multi-million USD project from MMRDA for infrastructure monitoring

News 11 July 2025

Scope entails consolidating multi-departmental data into a unified platform, enabling MMRDA to monitor project progress and performance in real time.

All India – BSE listed tech firm CS TECH Ai (Legal Name: Ceinsys Tech Ltd) has received a Letter of Award (LoA) from the Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority (MMRDA) for a technology integration project valued at $13 million (Rs 114.98 crore).

The project involves the selection of a System Integrator (SI) for monitoring infrastructure projects using the Integrated Data and Dashboard Platform (IDDP) developed by MMRDA.

The Nagpur headquartered tech firm, CS TECH Ai will be responsible for deploying end-to-end system integration and digital monitoring of ongoing and future infrastructure works under the authority's jurisdiction.

According to a regulatory filing, the total value of the contract is Rs 1,14,98,66,659. The company informed the BSE under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

The deployment is expected to enhance project tracking and streamline decision-making across infrastructure verticals handled by the MMRDA. CS TECH Ai will integrate data from multiple engineering and project execution departments and deliver a unified platform for real-time project status and performance metrics.

“This engagement reinforces the role of integrated platforms in transforming how infrastructure programs are monitored and managed. As data becomes central to governance, our focus is on enabling institutions like MMRDA to gain real-time digital intelligence, streamline coordination, and strengthen execution across complex, multi-stakeholder environments.”
– Prashant Kamat, CEO, CS TECH Ai (Ceinsys Tech)

This order adds to CS TECH Ai’s ongoing portfolio of digital transformation and engineering projects for government and urban development agencies.
