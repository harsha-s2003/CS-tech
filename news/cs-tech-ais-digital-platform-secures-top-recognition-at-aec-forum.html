CS TECH Ai’s Digital Platform Secures Top Recognition at AEC Forum

News 21 Aug 2025

A key milestone as the company gets recognized across forums while it continues to deliver strong revenue growth

New Delhi, August 21, 2025 – We are proud to share that CS TECH Ai has been awarded the AEC Excellence Award 2025 for Application Excellence at the AEC Forum in New Delhi.

This recognition was given to our Digital Project Management System (DPMS), a platform we built to enhance execution and oversight of large-scale water infrastructure projects. By integrating geospatial data, real-time dashboards, and mobile-enabled reporting, our solution provides stakeholders with visibility and accountability at every stage. Its application in water supply projects has been especially impactful, helping ensure millions of people gain equitable access to safe drinking water.

This achievement comes as CS TECH Ai continues to be acknowledged across diverse forums. After receiving the SME Champion Award for Excellence in Community Engagement, our leadership in water and energy sectors has once again been recognized at the AEC Forum, marking an important milestone in our growth journey.

The recognition also coincides with a period of strong performance. In Q1 FY26, our company delivered 112% year-on-year revenue growth, supported by a robust order book and several high-value project wins. These results highlight the momentum we are building and reinforce our role in shaping resilient, future-ready infrastructure through digital intelligence.

The award was jointly accepted by Mr. E Ravideran, IAS (Mission Director, JJM Maharashtra), Mr. Ranga Nayak, IAS (Member Secretary, MJP), and Dr. Abhay Kimmatkar (Managing Director, CS TECH Ai). Their presence underscored the importance of public–private collaboration in modern infrastructure governance.
