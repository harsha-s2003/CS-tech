CS TECH Ai Expands Global Footprint with Strategic MoU with Aetosky Singapore

News 5 Dec 2025

Delhi, India – December 5, 2025

CS TECH Ai has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Aetosky Singapore to jointly deliver enterprise-grade geospatial and AI-enabled infrastructure solutions across GCC, Europe, and North America.

The MoU was formally signed by Dr. Abhay Mittal and Sandip Banerjee from Aetosky, along with Dr. Abhay Kimmatkar and Rahul Joharapurkar from CS TECH Ai, marking a significant milestone in CS TECH Ai’s global expansion strategy.

Strengthening Global Infrastructure Digitization

This strategic collaboration is aimed at transforming how governments, utilities, and infrastructure owners plan, digitize, and manage complex infrastructure assets, particularly across transportation, water, and energy networks.

By combining CS TECH Ai’s advanced capabilities in:

  • Geospatial data acquisition
  • GIS engineering and analytics
  • Digital twin development
  • AI-driven automation and decision intelligence
  • With Aetosky’s strong regional presence, domain expertise, and client relationships, the partnership is positioned to deliver scalable, high-impact infrastructure solutions tailored for global markets.

Aligned with Long-Term Growth Vision

The MoU aligns closely with CS TECH Ai’s long-term vision of expanding its international presence, strengthening technology-led offerings, and building strategic partnerships that accelerate innovation in infrastructure management.

With increasing global demand for smart cities, resilient infrastructure, and data-driven governance, the collaboration is expected to unlock new opportunities across multiple geographies and sectors.

Looking Ahead

Both organizations share a common commitment to innovation, execution excellence, and sustainable infrastructure development. This partnership represents a step forward in enhancing possibilities through geospatial intelligence and AI.

As CS TECH Ai continues to scale its global operations, collaborations such as this reinforce its position as a trusted partner for next-generation infrastructure solutions worldwide.
