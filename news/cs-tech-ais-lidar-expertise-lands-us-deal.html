CS TECH Ai’s LiDAR expertise lands US deal

News 21 Feb 2024

Nagpur, Maharashtra, India, 21 February, 2024 – CS TECH Ai (Ceinsys Tech Ltd), a pioneer in providing Geospatial, IT, Engineering and Mobility services strikes a significant deal with a US based firm for LiDAR data feature extraction for the state of New Mexico.

CS TECH Ai’s expertise will aid in extracting the LiDAR assets features like linear & point pavements, sidewalks, bridge/structures, traffic signals, driveways, rail roads, fixed objects, lanes etc. CS TECH Ai will be using its in-house built Scan to Model AI platform for the automated feature extraction process. The platform will handle large datasets with high accuracy, ensuring a robust and efficient solution for automated feature extraction, saving time and resources.

Valued at approximately $0.6 Mn (INR 5.26 Crores), the project will be executed in 8 months.

CS TECH Ai is setting a new standard for LiDAR data processing with this landmark project. CS Tech AI’ LiDAR expertise and Scan to Model platform are poised to revolutionize various industries and contribute significantly to infrastructure development and economic growth. This prestigious project is a testament to CS TECH Ai’s commitment to leveraging technology and innovation to solve real-world problems and create a sustainable future for all.
