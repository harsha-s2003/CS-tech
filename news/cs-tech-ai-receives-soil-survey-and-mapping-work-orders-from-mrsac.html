CS TECH Ai Receives Soil Survey and Mapping Work Orders from MRSAC

News 29 September 2025

Project Under National Soil Mapping Programme to Support Scientific Land and Agricultural Planning

September 29, 2025 – CS TECH Ai (Ceinsys Tech Limited) has received work orders from the Maharashtra Remote Sensing Application Centre (MRSAC) for carrying out Detailed Soil Survey and Mapping under the National Soil Mapping Programme (NSMP). The project covers Gondia district and parts of Nashik district in Maharashtra and is valued at approximately ₹3.17 crore, including GST.

The company is empaneled with MRSAC for executing Detailed Soil Survey and Mapping projects under the NSMP framework. The latest work order further strengthens CS TECH Ai’s role in supporting government-led geospatial and agricultural initiatives through advanced mapping and data-driven technologies.

The National Soil Mapping Programme is aimed at developing scientific and accurate soil resource information that can support sustainable agricultural practices, land-use planning, irrigation management, and environmental conservation. Detailed soil mapping helps identify soil properties, fertility patterns, land capability, and crop suitability, enabling better decision-making for farmers, planners, and policymakers.

As part of the project, CS TECH Ai will utilize geospatial technologies, remote sensing tools, and digital mapping techniques to generate reliable soil-related data. Such information is essential for improving agricultural productivity, optimizing resource utilization, and supporting long-term rural and environmental development.

The project also reflects the growing importance of technology-enabled surveying and geospatial intelligence in modern governance and infrastructure planning. With increasing focus on sustainable land management and precision agriculture, initiatives such as NSMP are expected to play a critical role in strengthening the agricultural ecosystem across the country.

The order highlights the company’s continued expansion in the geospatial sector and reinforces its commitment to delivering high-quality technology solutions for land, infrastructure, and environmental management projects across India.
