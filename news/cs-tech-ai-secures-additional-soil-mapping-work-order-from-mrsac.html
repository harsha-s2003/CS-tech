CS TECH Ai Secures Additional Soil Mapping Work Order from MRSAC

News 19 November 2025

Strengthening Geospatial and Soil Survey Capabilities Under the National Soil Mapping Programme

November 19, 2025 – CS TECH Ai (Ceinsys tech limited) has received an additional work order from the Maharashtra Remote Sensing Application Centre (MRSAC) for conducting Detailed Soil Survey and Mapping under the National Soil Mapping Programme (NSMP). The newly awarded project covers Gondia district and a part of Nashik district in Maharashtra and is valued at ₹1.86 crore, including GST.

With this latest order, the total value of work orders received by the company from MRSAC now stands at approximately ₹5.04 crore, including GST, along with the earlier order received on September 29, 2025. This achievement further strengthens CS TECH Ai’s position as a trusted geospatial and survey solutions provider for large-scale government initiatives.

CS TECH Ai is empaneled with MRSAC for Detailed Soil Survey and Mapping projects under the NSMP framework. The programme aims to create accurate and scientific soil resource data that can support better agricultural planning, land management, and sustainable development. Detailed soil mapping plays an important role in identifying soil characteristics, improving crop suitability analysis, supporting irrigation planning, and enabling efficient use of natural resources.

The project will leverage advanced geospatial technologies, remote sensing capabilities, and data-driven mapping methodologies to deliver precise and reliable soil information. Such initiatives contribute significantly to strengthening agricultural productivity and informed decision-making for policymakers and stakeholders.

The company has also stated that the disclosure has been made in compliance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations and the relevant SEBI circulars issued from time to time regarding material events and disclosures.

This development highlights CS TECH Ai’s growing involvement in government-led digital and geospatial transformation programmes and reflects its continued commitment to delivering technology-enabled solutions for infrastructure, land, and environmental management.
