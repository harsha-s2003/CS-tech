02. EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

Engineering Intelligent and Connected Vehicle Systems

CS TECH Ai delivers embedded systems solutions that combine hardware and software engineering expertise to optimize product development and accelerate time-to-market.

Infotainment

Engineering and development services for automotive infotainment systems, focusing on user experience, interface development and software integration.

  • Middleware Development and Legacy Migration
  • Android-, QNX-, ROS- and iOS-Based Systems
Body Control

Development of electronic control solutions supporting vehicle access, comfort, convenience and safety-related functions.

  • Automated Wiper, Lighting and Seat Adjustment Systems
  • Electronic Steering Lock Controls
ADAS & Safety

Embedded software engineering, simulation and validation services supporting advanced driver assistance systems and software-defined vehicles.

  • Application Development and Embedded Programming using Python, C and C++

Telematics & In-Vehicle Network

Software and hardware engineering for vehicle communication networks, domain controllers and connected vehicle technologies.

  • DSRC and Firmware Over-the-Air (FOTA) Solutions
