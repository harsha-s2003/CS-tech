CORE REALITY CAPTURE SERVICES

Four Core Capture Methods. One Integrated Reality Capture Ecosystem.

The right method depends on the environment — and some projects require more than one.

Aerial LiDAR

High-accuracy 3D capture for large areas, terrain, vegetation and linear infrastructure.

  • Utility & transmission corridors
  • Floodplain & drainage mapping
  • Topographic mapping
  • Transportation corridors
  • Large-area infrastructure mapping
Key Outputs

Point cloud | DTM/DSM | Contours | Planimetrics | PLS-CADD | CAD / GIS

Aerial Photogrammetry

High-resolution imagery and 3D outputs for mapping, visual context, monitoring and planning.

  • Construction monitoring
  • Site documentation
  • Asset documentation
  • Progress tracking
  • Open-site & land-development
Key Outputs

Orthomosaic | DSM | DTM | Point cloud | 3D models | Contours | CAD / GIS

Mobile Mapping

Vehicle-based LiDAR and imaging for rapid capture of transportation, utility and telecom assets.

  • Transportation corridor mapping
  • Utility & distribution networks
  • Broadband / fiber mapping
  • Roadway documentation
  • Municipal infrastructure
  • Asset inventory
Key Outputs

Point cloud | Imagery | Asset data | Corridor datasets | CAD / GIS

Terrestrial Laser Scanning

High-detail 3D capture for dense, obstructed and high-accuracy environments.

  • Industrial plant as-builts
  • Offshore platforms
  • Oil & gas facilities
  • Energy / power facilities
  • Brownfield / retrofit projects
Key Outputs

Registered point cloud | CAD drawings | 3D models | As-builts | BIM-ready data
