CORE CAPABILITIES

One Platform for Project Configuration, Inspections and Field Surveys

Configure projects, organize teams, manage stage hierarchies, run inspections and monitor field operations with a unified web and mobile platform.

Project Configuration

Create and manage projects with site details, descriptions and GPS-based locations.

User Onboarding & RBAC

Invite team members, assign projects and control access through role-based permissions.

Stage Hierarchy Management

Configure multi-level stages and sub-stages to organize large or complex project structures.

Inspection Templates & Approval Workflows

Build scheme, compliance and bill inspection templates with configurable checklists and multi-level approvals.

Work Assignments

Supervisors can assign field work or any task to reportees with target dates, track assignment status, and monitor completion.

GIS Map View & Visibility

Visualize project sites, inspections and field activities on an interactive geographic map.

Mobile Inspection, Timesheet & Offline Support

Capture observations, photos, documents, GPS attendance and drafts from the field, even with limited connectivity.

Progress Monitoring

Monitor physical and financial progress of the whole project based on configured criteria, with stage-wise and overall progress visibility.

Dashboards & Reports

Access project dashboards, track inspection and project status, review history, and generate/share downloadable PDF-ready reports.
