CASE STUDY

Electrical Distribution Network Modernisation

The Assignment

Large electrical distribution networks require accurate, connected and up-to-date information on network infrastructure, assets and consumers to support effective planning and management.

The assignment involved large-scale electrical network mapping and infrastructure data development, creating a geospatial foundation to support the digital modernisation of distribution networks.

The programme experience covered 65,000+ miles (approximately 104,000+ km) of network mapping, 5,000+ assets and infrastructure benefiting approximately 10 million consumers.

Our Approach

CS TECH Ai applied GIS-based utility mapping and data-management capabilities to create structured, location-enabled information for electrical distribution infrastructure.

The engagement included:

  • Large-scale electrical network mapping
  • GIS-based development and management of network information
  • Mapping and documentation of distribution assets
  • Creation of structured asset and network databases
  • Development of georeferenced information to support utility planning
  • Consumer indexing and linkage of consumers with network infrastructure
  • Management of consumer attributes and connectivity information
  • Establishment of traceability across transformers, feeders, poles and circuits
  • Creation of a reliable geospatial foundation for network planning and management

CS TECH Ai's GIS-based Consumer Indexing approach enables utilities to connect consumer information with the corresponding network components, providing greater visibility of relationships across the distribution system.

Impact

Established structured geospatial information for large-scale electrical distribution networks.
Improved visibility of network infrastructure, assets and connectivity.
Supported better planning and management of distribution networks.
Enabled consumers to be geographically linked with associated network components.
Created a reliable digital foundation for utility network modernisation.
Supported infrastructure serving approximately 10 million consumers.
