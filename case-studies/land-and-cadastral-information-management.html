CASE STUDY

Land & Cadastral Information Management

Land & Natural Resources Geospatial Data Processing & Mapping

The Assignment

Land administration requires accurate, structured and accessible information about land parcels, boundaries and associated records. When cadastral information exists across fragmented datasets or legacy systems, it can become difficult for government agencies and enterprises to maintain reliable land information and use it effectively across planning, administration and operational workflows.

The assignment involved transforming geospatial and cadastral data into structured land and parcel information that could support land administration and wider enterprise use.

Our Approach

CS TECH Ai applied geospatial data processing, cadastral mapping and parcel-generation capabilities to create structured, location-based land information.

The engagement included:

  • Cadastral mapping and digitisation of land information
  • Generation and delineation of land parcels
  • Processing and structuring of geospatial datasets
  • Georeferencing of imagery and mapping information
  • Collection and use of Ground Control Points through DGPS surveys, where required
  • Integration of spatial and associated land information
  • Cadastral data management and parcel modernisation
  • Preparation of structured land datasets for enterprise use
  • Development of information that can support government and organisational land-management workflows
  • Creation of accurate geospatial information for planning, administration and decision-making

The approach transformed raw and fragmented spatial information into structured parcel-level data that could be maintained, accessed and used within wider land-information and enterprise environments.

Impact

Improved availability of structured and location-based land and parcel information.
Supported more efficient management and use of cadastral datasets.
Enabled land information to be integrated into wider government and enterprise workflows.
Improved access to geospatial information for planning and administrative decision-making.
Supported modernisation of cadastral and parcel-management processes.
Helped customers improve operational efficiency and reduce the costs associated with managing and using geospatial information.
