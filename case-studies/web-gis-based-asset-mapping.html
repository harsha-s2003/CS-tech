CASE STUDY

Web-GIS Based Asset Mapping and Land Management for Government Facilities

About the Client

A state government department responsible for managing and monitoring public land assets across districts, including educational institutions, administrative buildings, and civic infrastructure. The client required a solution to digitally document, analyze, and govern land usage effectively.

The Challenge

The client was managing thousands of land parcels across multiple zones without centralized visibility or spatial intelligence. Records were scattered, legacy systems were outdated, and manual tracking made it difficult to prevent encroachments, validate ownership, and plan resource use effectively.

The Solution

We developed a Web-GIS based land asset management system that enabled centralized monitoring and spatial analysis of government-owned land.

Key components included:

  • Geo-tagging and mapping of public land parcels
  • Ownership validation using cadastral overlays and field verification
  • Attribute tagging of land use, boundaries, and infrastructure
  • Development of a responsive Web-GIS dashboard with user-role access
  • Integration with revenue, planning, and audit departments
  • Provision of real-time land status insights and decision-support tools

The Impact

The solution empowered the department with complete digital oversight of land assets:

Prevented unauthorized usage and reduced encroachment incidents
Improved inter-departmental coordination through a unified data layer
Enabled transparent audit trails and status reporting
Supported informed planning and allocation decisions for infrastructure programs
Created a replicable framework for use across other states and agencies
