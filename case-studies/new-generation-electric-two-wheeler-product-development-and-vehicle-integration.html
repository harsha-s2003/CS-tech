CASE STUDY

New-Generation Electric Two-Wheeler – Product Development & Vehicle Integration

Automotive & Mobility Product & Manufacturing Engineering

The Assignment

A leading electric two-wheeler manufacturer required engineering support for the development of a new-generation electric vehicle platform, covering multiple vehicle systems from concept development through detailed engineering and validation.

The assignment required an integrated approach to vehicle development, bringing together packaging, structural systems, battery integration, powertrain, suspension, steering and other vehicle subsystems while addressing overall vehicle-level requirements and production readiness.

Our Approach

CS TECH Ai provided product development and vehicle integration engineering support across key systems of the electric two-wheeler platform.

The engagement included:

  • Vehicle architecture, integration and packaging
  • Chassis and structural system engineering
  • Battery packaging and integration
  • Electric powertrain integration
  • Suspension system engineering
  • Steering system development and integration
  • Lighting system integration
  • Component packaging and interface management
  • 3D CAD development and detailed engineering
  • Design validation and engineering optimisation
  • Engineering support towards production readiness

The integrated engineering approach enabled individual vehicle systems to be developed and packaged within the overall vehicle architecture while addressing interfaces, space constraints and functional requirements across the platform.

Impact

Supported development of a new-generation electric two-wheeler platform through integrated product engineering.
Enabled coordinated engineering across chassis, battery, powertrain, suspension, steering and lighting systems.
Supported vehicle-level packaging and integration across multiple interconnected systems.
Advanced the programme from engineering development and detailed design towards design validation and production readiness.
Provided multidisciplinary engineering support across key stages of electric vehicle product development.
