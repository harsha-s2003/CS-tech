CASE STUDY

Wireless Battery Management System

Automotive & Mobility Emerging Tech

About the Client

An emerging player in the electric mobility space, focused on delivering sustainable and intelligent two-/three-wheeler electric vehicles for urban, rural, fleet, and last-mile delivery. Their mission blends performance, affordability, and digital innovation to accelerate the shift to green transport.

The Challenge

Challenges with traditional wired systems included:

  • Wiring Complexity – Adds weight and failure points
  • Manufacturing Overhead – Time-consuming and manual
  • Maintenance Difficulty – Hard to diagnose and service
  • Low Modularity – Inflexible for scaling or reuse
  • EMI Issues – Requires shielding and design tuning
  • Stress Impact – Thermal and vibration affect reliability
  • Limited Cybersecurity – Vulnerable diagnostic interfaces
  • Not Ideal for Swappable Batteries – Connector wear and integration issues
  • Platform-Dependent Architecture – Limits reuse across models

The Solution

CS TECH Ai delivered a robust telemetry-enabled wireless solution for real-time battery monitoring, analysis, and control.

  • Wireless architecture eliminates complex wiring, reduces weight
  • Swappable battery support and cross-platform compatibility
  • Platform-agnostic design adaptable across: Two-wheelers Three-wheelers Light commercial vehicles Off-highway vehicles

The Impact

Reduces wiring complexity and weight
Simplifies manufacturing processes
Enhances modular and swappable battery capabilities
Supports real-time telemetry and diagnostics
Improves system reliability and uptime
Enables platform-agnostic EV designs
Lowers cost and accelerates market readiness
