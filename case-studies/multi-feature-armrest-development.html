CASE STUDY

Multi-Feature Armrest Development for Global Luxury Passenger Vehicle Platform

About the Client

A European luxury passenger car OEM designing feature-rich interior components for global markets. The client required a multi-functional front and rear armrest that balanced comfort, utility, and cost, while adhering to ergonomic and regulatory guidelines.

The Challenge

The project involved the design of a multi-functional, ergonomically optimized armrest with premium and tech-integrated features. The design needed to meet varied market expectations and integrate seamlessly with the overall interior design language of the vehicle.

Key challenges included:

  • Balancing multi-functionality with cost and packaging constraints
  • Ensuring ergonomic access and comfort for rear-seat passengers
  • Designing modular variants for multiple trims and geographies
  • Meeting regulatory, safety, and manufacturability requirements

The Solution

We executed the armrest development with a modular approach, delivering both base and premium variants aligned with the vehicle’s design and functional goals.

Key solution components included:

  • Tray with integrated storage for small items (coins, pen, paper)
  • Deployable cupholders and tablet/iPad holder with complex mechanisms
  • Integrated headrest and sliding lid functionality
  • Modular platform enabling base and premium feature configurations
  • Premium variant features such as: Glass deco part USB charging ports Wireless charging module Perforated leather lid
  • Ergonomic validation to ensure ease of access and user-friendly operation
  • Seamless integration with the vehicle’s interior systems and trim architecture

The Impact

The project delivered a well-balanced solution blending comfort, luxury, and functional versatility:

Successful deployment of multiple armrest variants across global markets
Enhanced passenger comfort with smart, easy-to-use features
Improved cost control through modularity and carryover compatibility
Client trust strengthened, leading to follow-up programs for premium interior components
Enabled faster adaptation to different vehicle configurations and market needs
