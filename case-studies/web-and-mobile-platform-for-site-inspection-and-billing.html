CASE STUDY

Web & Mobile Platform for Site Inspection & Billing

Telecom Emerging Tech

The Assignment

Site inspection and billing processes involve multiple stakeholders, field activities, validations and approvals. Managing these activities efficiently requires information captured at site to move through a structured workflow from inspection through review and final bill confirmation.

CS TECH Ai developed a web and mobile-based platform to support site inspections and billing through an integrated digital workflow. The solution brought field engineers, principal engineers, administrators, customers and vendors onto a common platform for managing inspection, compliance, approvals and billing activities.

Our Approach

The platform was designed around the complete site-inspection workflow, enabling field information to be captured, validated, reviewed and progressed through defined approval stages.

Field engineers could visit sites, conduct inspections, geotag locations, capture detailed images and validate inspection data. Principal engineers could manage workloads, assign inspections and regions, and review or revise submitted information.

Administrative workflows supported customer data creation, user management, final approvals and bill confirmation.

The solution supported:

  • Site inspection and field-data capture
  • Geotagging of locations and associated information
  • Detailed image capture
  • Inspection-data validation
  • Compliance validation
  • Workload and regional assignment
  • Review and approval workflows
  • User and role management
  • Bill validation and confirmation
  • Progress monitoring and dashboarding

Technology & Delivery

The solution combined web and mobile interfaces to connect field activities with centralized project, inspection and billing workflows.

Role-based processes enabled different stakeholders to participate at relevant stages of the workflow, from site inspection and compliance validation through review, approval and final bill confirmation.

The resulting workflow provided a structured digital process:

Site Inspection → Geotagging & Image Capture → Data & Compliance Validation → Review & Approval → Bill Confirmation → Progress Monitoring

Impact

Digitized the workflow from field inspection through billing and approval.
Connected field teams, engineering reviewers, administrators, customers and vendors through a common process.
Enabled geotagged site information and detailed images to support inspection records.
Established structured validation and approval workflows for inspection and compliance information.
Provided centralized progress monitoring and dashboarding across the inspection process.
