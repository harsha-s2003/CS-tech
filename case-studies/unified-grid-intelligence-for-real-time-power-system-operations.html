CASE STUDY

Unified Grid Intelligence for Real-Time Power System Operations

About the Client

The Maharashtra State Load Despatch Centre (MSLDC) plays a critical role in monitoring and managing power-system operations across Maharashtra.

Its operating environment involves information generated across multiple grid, market, metering and renewable-energy systems, creating a need for a unified view of operational data to support monitoring, analysis and informed decision-making.

The Challenge

Power-system operations generate large volumes of information across different operational and enterprise platforms. At MSLDC, relevant information was distributed across systems including SCADA, SAMAST, REMC, MDM, DSM and WRPC data sources.

Working across these separate systems made it difficult to establish a consolidated operational view and efficiently analyse information across different datasets.

MSLDC therefore required an integrated data environment capable of bringing together information from multiple IT and OT sources and making it readily available for operational monitoring, analysis and reporting.

The Solution

CS TECH Ai worked on a Proof of Value for a Unified Grid Intelligence environment using the AVEVA PI System, creating a centralized operational-data framework for integrating and visualizing information from multiple power-system sources.

The solution brought together data from systems including SCADA, SAMAST, REMC, MDM, DSM and WRPC, providing a common environment for operational information.

The approach supported:

  • Centralized IT-OT data integration across multiple operational systems
  • Consolidation of grid and related operational information within a common data environment
  • Real-time monitoring and visualization of relevant operational parameters
  • Historical data access and analysis
  • Demand and grid-operation analysis
  • Monitoring and analysis of renewable-energy information
  • Operational dashboards and analytical views
  • Data availability for regulatory and operational reporting
  • A common information foundation to support further operational analytics and decision-making

By using the AVEVA PI System as the operational-data infrastructure, information originating in otherwise separate systems could be organized and presented within a unified operational context.

The Impact

The Proof of Value demonstrated how a unified grid-intelligence environment could give MSLDC a more consolidated view of its operational information and reduce dependence on individually accessing multiple source systems.

The integrated approach enabled:

Improved visibility across grid and operational datasets
A common environment for analysing information from multiple IT and OT systems
Easier access to current and historical operational information
Better support for demand analysis and renewable-energy monitoring
More streamlined access to information required for operational and regulatory reporting
A stronger data foundation for grid analysis and operational decision support

The result was a framework for transforming fragmented power-system information into connected operational intelligence, helping grid operators obtain a more comprehensive view of system conditions and supporting more informed power-system management.
