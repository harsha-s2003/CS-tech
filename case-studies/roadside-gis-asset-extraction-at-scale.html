CASE STUDY

Roadside GIS Asset Extraction at Scale

The Assignment

Large road networks generate substantial volumes of LiDAR, imagery and point-cloud data containing valuable information about roadside infrastructure. Converting this raw data into structured GIS information requires individual assets to be identified, attributed and made available for downstream mapping and asset-management workflows.

CS TECH Ai deployed its Scan-to-Model (S2M) platform to support large-scale roadside GIS asset extraction and attribution. The programme covered 11,450 miles of roadway and 48 attribution types, demonstrating the ability to process road-corridor information at significant scale.

Our Approach

The workflow combines point-cloud and image processing with manual and AI-driven feature extraction to convert registered scan data into structured, GIS-compatible information.

The engagement included:

  • Processing of registered scan and point-cloud data
  • Visualization of 3D point clouds and panoramic imagery
  • Manual and AI-driven roadside feature extraction
  • Identification of roadside point features
  • Capture of asset location and dimensions
  • Point and line attribution
  • Creation and management of user-defined GIS layers
  • Intensity-based line extraction
  • Curb and gutter-line extraction
  • Road-marking and symbol extraction
  • Image linking and map integration
  • QA/QC of extracted information
  • Export of information into GIS-compatible formats

S2M supports integration with GIS environments including ArcGIS and QGIS, allowing extracted information to move into established geospatial workflows.

Project Scale

  • 11,450 miles of attribution completed
  • 48 attribution types covered

These figures come directly from a completed project dashboard documented within the S2M programme.

AI-Enabled Feature Extraction

AI-driven automation forms an important part of the processing workflow.

The platform supports automatic identification of roadside point features, including their location and dimensions, while additional automated workflows support extraction of linear and surface-related information.

Capabilities demonstrated within the S2M environment include:

  • AI-based asset detection
  • Intensity-based automatic extraction
  • Automatic curb and gutter extraction
  • Automatic powerline extraction
  • Road-marking and symbol extraction
  • OCR-supported asset detection
  • Image linking
  • Automated labelling
  • QA/QC tools

The underlying AI model demonstrated in the source material supports 15+ object types across imagery and LiDAR-based workflows.

From Raw Scan Data to GIS-Ready Information

The processing workflow can be represented as:

Registered Scan Data → Processing & Visualization → Manual / AI Feature Extraction → Attribution → QA/QC → GIS-Compatible Outputs

The platform is designed to manage point-cloud and image information within collaborative project environments, with work allocation, layer management, measurements, labelling and feature creation brought together within the same workflow.

Impact

Completed roadside attribution across 11,450 miles.
Covered 48 different attribution types within the programme.
Converted large volumes of scan and imagery data into structured GIS information.
Combined manual processing with AI-driven automation for feature extraction.
Enabled roadside assets to be identified together with their location and dimensions.
Supported automated extraction of curbs, gutters, road markings and other infrastructure features.
Provided QA/QC and GIS integration within the processing workflow.
Created GIS-compatible outputs for subsequent mapping and asset-information applications.
