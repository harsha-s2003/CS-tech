CASE STUDY

Third-Party Inspection Application for Infrastructure Quality Assurance

Water & Wastewater Transportation Infrastructure Emerging Tech Engineering & Consulting

About the Client

The client is a private-sector consulting organization operating as a third-party inspection (TPI) agency. They specialize in independent quality assurance for infrastructure projects commissioned by government bodies.

The client's mandate involves inspecting civil infrastructure components such as water supply networks, pumping stations, storage tanks, and roads. Their core responsibility is to ensure project compliance with technical, safety, and quality standards as per government guidelines.

The Challenge

The client faced significant operational challenges in managing inspections across geographically dispersed locations. The manual and partially digital inspection processes led to inefficiencies, such as:

  • Delayed reporting from field engineers
  • Inconsistent data formats across various inspection types (e.g., work, safety, billing, JMR)
  • Lack of real-time visibility into inspection status and compliance
  • Difficulty in tracking and validating photographic evidence from the field
  • Redundant communication loops for approvals and escalation

These gaps introduced risks in ensuring accountability and hindered the timely decision-making necessary for large-scale government infrastructure audits.

The Solution

CS TECH Ai developed a customized third-party inspection platform comprising a mobile and web application suite to digitize and streamline the client's inspection lifecycle.

Key Solution Highlights:

  • Mobile Application for Field Engineers
  • On-site inspection data capture
  • Multiple inspection formats: Work, Safety, JMR, Billing
  • Image capture with automated geotagging
  • Field-level compliance checklist integration

Hierarchical Workflow:

  • Seamless data escalation from Field Engineer to Principal Engineer, District Team, and Admin
  • Role-based access and actions: Accept, Reject, Comment

Web Dashboard for Admin & Review Teams:

  • Real-time tracking of inspections across all types and locations
  • Search, filter, and review capabilities for compliance and status
  • Visual dashboards with inspection metrics and trends

Technologies Used:

  • React (Web UI)
  • Flutter (Mobile App)
  • Spring Boot (Backend API)
  • PostgreSQL (Database)
  • AWS S3 (Image storage)
  • Geo-tagging API integration
  • Authentication & Role Management Module

The Impact

The solution significantly improved the client's TPI operations by:

40% reduction in inspection-to-approval cycle time
Real-time visibility into field activity across districts
Consistent compliance documentation across inspection types
Improved accountability through geotagged evidence and user action trails
Enabling the client to handle 30% more inspections per month without increasing manpower
Enhanced trust and transparency with the government stakeholder due to structured and auditable data
