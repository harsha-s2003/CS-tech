CASE STUDY

Unified GIS for Statewide Power Distribution Network Management

Power & Energy Enterprise GIS & Spatial Intelligence

About the Client

Uttar Pradesh Power Corporation Limited (UPPCL) is responsible for power distribution across Uttar Pradesh through its distribution companies, serving an extensive electrical network spread across urban and rural areas.

Managing infrastructure at this scale requires reliable spatial information on the electricity network, assets and consumers to support planning, operations, asset management and informed decision-making.

The Challenge

Electrical-network information was distributed across different systems and datasets, creating a need for a consistent and unified geospatial view of the distribution network.

The programme required large-scale surveying and digitisation of electrical infrastructure, along with accurate indexing of network assets and consumers. The resulting GIS information also needed to connect with relevant enterprise and operational systems so that it could support broader utility workflows rather than function as an isolated mapping database.

A key requirement was therefore to establish a reliable spatial information foundation capable of supporting network visibility, asset management, consumer indexing, energy-audit requirements and operational planning.

The Solution

CS TECH Ai supported the development and enhancement of a Unified GIS environment for UPPCL, combining extensive field surveying, electrical-network mapping, GIS database development and enterprise-system integration.

The engagement included:

  • Large-scale topographic and electrical-network surveys
  • Mapping and digitisation of distribution infrastructure
  • Asset indexing and consumer tagging to establish relationships between consumers and the electrical network
  • GPS/mobile-based field data collection and asset photography
  • Structured GIS database creation and network modelling
  • Data verification, QA/QC and migration
  • Development and enhancement of Web GIS applications and dashboards
  • GIS-based visualization and management of electrical assets
  • Integration of GIS information with relevant utility and enterprise applications

The solution created a common geospatial environment through which network, asset and consumer information could be visualised, queried and used across utility functions.

The programme covered electrical-network surveying and mapping across multiple UPPCL distribution-company areas and included both IPDS and R-APDRP towns, making it a substantial GIS and network-digitisation exercise.

The Impact

The Unified GIS environment established a stronger digital foundation for managing UPPCL's extensive distribution infrastructure and provided greater visibility into the relationship between network assets, geographic locations and consumers.

The programme supported:

Improved visibility of the electrical distribution network
More structured and accessible asset and consumer information
Better asset traceability and field-to-database linkage
Support for consumer indexing and energy-audit workflows
Improved network planning and infrastructure-management capabilities
GIS-based information for operational and management decision-making
A more reliable spatial-data foundation for integration with other utility systems
Long-term digital management of electrical-network assets

The result was a more connected approach to distribution-network information, transforming field-survey and asset data into a unified enterprise GIS resource for planning, operations and asset management.
