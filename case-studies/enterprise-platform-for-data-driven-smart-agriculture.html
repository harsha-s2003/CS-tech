CASE STUDY

Enterprise Platform for Data-Driven Smart Agriculture

About the Client

A state agriculture department supporting farmers, agri-extension workers, and research institutions through planning, monitoring, and delivery of agricultural services across multiple regions. The client sought a centralized digital solution to improve productivity and operational efficiency.

The Challenge

The department was managing diverse agricultural data—soil, crop, weather, pest control—through disconnected systems, manual records, and legacy workflows. This hindered insight generation, slowed service delivery, and limited the ability to run targeted, evidence-based programs at scale.

The Solution

We developed an enterprise-grade digital agriculture platform to centralize, analyze, and deliver actionable insights to field teams, farmers, and administrators.

Key capabilities included:

  • Field data collection through mobile apps integrated with GIS
  • Centralized data store for soil health, crop patterns, and weather
  • Role-based dashboards for government, research, and agri-extension staff
  • Alerts and advisories based on pest detection and weather conditions
  • Integration of IoT sensors and satellite imagery for real-time monitoring
  • WebGIS interface for land parcel visualization and analytics

The Impact

The solution strengthened data-driven decision-making and delivery efficiency across the state:

Improved visibility into farm-level conditions and interventions
Faster response to crop and pest-related risks
Better planning of seed distribution, irrigation, and subsidy schemes
Reduced data duplication and manual errors
Scalable framework now adopted by other departments and states
