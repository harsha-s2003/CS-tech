CASE STUDY

Advanced Metering & Network Mapping for 10M Consumers

About the Client

A large electricity distribution environment serving approximately 10 million consumers, requiring comprehensive network mapping and structured geospatial information to support advanced metering and utility operations.

The Challenge

Managing an electricity distribution network serving 10 million consumers requires accurate and consistently structured information about network infrastructure, assets and consumers.

The programme required a reliable geospatial foundation that could connect distribution-network assets with consumer information and provide spatial support for advanced metering initiatives. Accurate network mapping and consumer indexing were therefore critical to improving network visibility and enabling utility teams to work with consistent, location-based information.

The Solution

CS TECH Ai supported the programme through GIS network mapping, asset database creation, consumer indexing and spatial enablement for advanced metering.

The engagement included:

  • GIS-based mapping of more than 65,000 miles of electricity network
  • Creation and structuring of network and asset information
  • Mapping and management of information covering 5,000+ assets
  • Consumer indexing and spatial association of consumer records
  • Spatial enablement supporting advanced metering across approximately 10 million consumers
  • Creation of GIS-ready information to support utility planning and operational workflows

The resulting geospatial environment provided a structured spatial foundation connecting distribution infrastructure, assets and consumer information.

The Impact

The programme established a large-scale geospatial information base supporting distribution-network management and advanced metering initiatives across a 10-million-consumer environment.

It improved visibility of the electricity distribution network, strengthened the spatial relationship between network assets and consumer information, and provided more consistent geospatial data for planning and operational activities. With 65,000+ miles of network mapped and 5,000+ assets represented, the programme created a stronger information foundation for utility-management and advanced-metering workflows.
