CASE STUDY

Project Formulation DPR – Urban & Rural

The Assignment

Government agencies required Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) for urban and rural water supply and wastewater schemes across multiple states and National & State programmes.

The assignment involved engineering surveys, hydraulic design, cost estimation and technical documentation to support the planning and development of sustainable water infrastructure across Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Uttar Pradesh and other locations.

Our Approach

CS TECH Ai provided multidisciplinary engineering and consultancy support covering the key stages of project formulation.

The engagement included:

  • Engineering surveys and field investigations
  • Hydraulic modelling and design
  • Preparation of Detailed Project Reports (DPRs)
  • Project estimates and engineering documentation
  • Urban and rural water supply planning
  • Wastewater consultancy
  • Project Management Consultancy support

The work combined field-level information, engineering analysis and infrastructure planning to develop implementation-ready project documentation for different water and wastewater schemes.

Technology & Delivery

The project brought together engineering surveys, hydraulic modelling, design, estimation and project-planning expertise within an integrated DPR-development workflow.

This approach supported project formulation across diverse urban and rural water infrastructure requirements and multiple government programmes.

Impact

Delivered DPR consultancy covering 7,500+ sq. km.
Supported planning across approximately 60 lakh properties.
Prepared DPRs and capital schemes representing ₹6,000+ crore in project value.
Supported urban and rural water supply and wastewater planning across multiple Indian states.
Created structured engineering and project documentation to support subsequent implementation of water infrastructure schemes.
