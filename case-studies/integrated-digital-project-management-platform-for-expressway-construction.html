CASE STUDY

Integrated Digital Project Management Platform for Expressway Construction

Transportation Infrastructure Engineering & Consulting

About the Client

A public-sector agency tasked with planning, developing, and overseeing high-value expressway infrastructure projects across diverse terrain, including tunnels and viaducts. The client sought a centralized digital platform to enhance end-to-end project visibility, streamline construction workflows, and ensure timely decision-making across stakeholders and contractors.

The Challenge

Large-scale expressway projects involve high complexity across land acquisition, construction, and operations—especially in hilly terrain with tunnels and viaducts. The client required a centralized digital solution to track engineering, procurement, and execution data across multiple phases, teams, and contractors. Manual tracking methods were delaying decision-making and inflating costs.

The Solution

We developed and deployed a Digital Project Management Platform (DPMP) combining GIS, BIM, ERP, and Analytics within a Common Data Environment (CDE) framework. Key components included a custom-built web application, real-time dashboards, 3D visualization, and integration with existing tools.

Key features:

  • GIS-based interface with high-resolution drone imagery
  • Integration of 4D/5D BIM with ERP systems
  • Real-time project status monitoring via command center dashboards
  • 3D digital twin for tunnels, viaducts, road alignment, and utilities

The Impact

Reduced time and cost overruns by enabling early issue detection
Created a single source of truth for all stakeholders
Digitized land acquisition tracking and compliance reporting
Improved coordination and transparency across project teams
