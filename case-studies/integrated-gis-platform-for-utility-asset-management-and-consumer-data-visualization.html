CASE STUDY

Integrated GIS Platform for Utility Asset Management and Consumer Data Visualization

About the Client

A regional electricity utility responsible for power distribution.

The Challenge

The client faced challenges with fragmented legacy systems that hindered centralized oversight, automated auditing, and effective consumer engagement. Key issues included:

  • Migrating legacy electrical network and consumer datasets to modern platforms
  • Absence of centralized asset visualization and data integration
  • Lack of integration between GIS, ERP, and SCADA systems
  • Disconnected systems for energy auditing, consumer grievance, and meter data management

The Solution

We developed a unified, rule-based GIS platform utilizing the ESRI ArcFM environment. Key components included:

  • Migration of legacy data to ESRI ArcFM 10.2.1
  • Rule-based electrical distribution data modeling
  • Comprehensive field surveys for asset mapping
  • Development of a web-based GIS application
  • Integration with SAP modules, Energy Audit systems, SCADA, and MDAS
  • Dashboards for performance monitoring and network analysis
  • Onsite deployment with user training

The Impact

The implementation led to significant improvements:

Up to 15% reduction in distribution losses
Increased revenue through improved billing efficiency
Reduction in theft-prone zones via data-driven planning
Enhanced customer satisfaction due to faster issue resolution
Established a foundation for future scalability and integration with emerging grid technologies
