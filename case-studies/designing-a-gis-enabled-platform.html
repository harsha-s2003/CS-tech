CASE STUDY

Designing a GIS-Enabled Platform for Property and Tax Information Management

Land & Natural Resources Enterprise GIS & Spatial Intelligence

About the Client

An urban local body responsible for municipal governance and revenue generation through property taxation. The client aimed to streamline property records and build a central system to improve assessment accuracy, increase collections, and enhance citizen transparency.

The Challenge

The municipality lacked a unified, spatially aware property database. Inconsistent records, outdated maps, and fragmented systems led to under-assessment, disputes, and revenue losses. Officials required a technology-backed solution to capture, analyze, and manage property data in real time.

The Solution

We delivered a GIS-integrated platform designed to digitize property records and integrate taxation workflows for greater operational control and citizen service delivery.

Key components included:

  • Property mapping using high-resolution satellite imagery and field surveys
  • Spatial linking of properties with ownership, usage, and tax assessment data
  • Customized dashboards for municipal users and tax officers
  • Integration with existing revenue and billing systems
  • Workflow automation for assessment, updates, and issue resolution
  • WebGIS access for internal departments and public portals for citizens

The Impact

The system created a comprehensive digital backbone for property and tax management:

Improved coverage and accuracy of property tax assessments
Enhanced transparency with citizen-facing self-service options
Reduced time for issue resolution and ownership verification
Strengthened revenue collection with spatial intelligence
Scalable architecture supporting future modules and data layers
